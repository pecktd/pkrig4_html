class FootRoll(heel: Transform | str, inner: Transform | str, outer: Transform | str)

Bases: LimbEnd

A foot after the IK end: ankle, ball and toe joints, rolled by pivots under the IK control.

The IK control gets:

  • roll: below 0 rocks back on the heel; up to rollBreak lifts the heel around the ball; beyond that the whole foot rolls onto the toe tip.
  • bank: above 0 rolls onto the outer edge, below 0 onto the inner edge.
  • heelTwist, toeTwist: turn the foot around the heel or the toe tip.
  • toeBend: bends the toes up (above 0) or down.

The limb's parts after the IK end must be the ball and the toe tip. Roles: ball_ik_handle, toe_ik_handle.

Args
heel
Guide at the back of the heel, on the ground.
inner
Guide on the inner edge of the foot.
outer
Guide on the outer edge of the foot.
