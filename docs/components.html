A digitigrade leg: upper leg, knee, hock, ankle, ball and toe joints. Starts in IK.

Like Leg, with a hock between the knee and the ankle, solved by a FlexSolver: flex_ctrl at the ankle swings the hock around it, and the stretch attributes are upperStretch, middleStretch and lowerStretch. The knee and the hock each get a bend control.

Bases: Component

A joint chain with one FK control per bone.

With tip=True (the default) the last guide gets a joint but no control: it marks the end of the last bone, so the chain has one control fewer than joints, like a tail or a finger. With tip=False every joint gets a control.

Each control except the last (or every control, with a tip) gets a stretch attribute: 1 makes the bone it drives 10% longer, -1 10% shorter. Stretch scales the bone's rest offset, so it works whichever axis the chain points down.

Roles: jnt (indexed), fk_ctrl (indexed), tip_jnt.

Uniform scale for the control shapes.

Parent of the component's root group, where the controls live.

Parent for the first joint; defaults to the root group.

Bases: Component

A head control turning the head joint, and eyes that look at a target.

The head joint turns with the control. With a neck, the control gets fkIk: in FK (0) the neck stays with its own control and the head joint follows the control's translation off the neck's tip; in IK (1) the neck aims at the head control and stretches towards it, and the head joint stays on the neck's tip. minStretch and maxStretch (0.9 and 1.1 times the neck's rest length) limit that stretch, so in IK the head stops short of a control pulled too far; FK ignores them. Without a neck, the head joint follows the control's translation.

child_space follows the head joint; the eyes hang from it, and so should anything else that moves with the head. twist is the head joint's turn around its Y axis relative to its parent joint; with a neck it drives Neck.head_twist.

Each eye has a control that turns it on top of the aim (<side>_eye_ctrl) and its own target under eye_target, which sits in front of the eyes and follows the head unless eye_spaces picks another space.

Roles: ctrl, jnt, tip_jnt, child_space, eye_target, and per eye eye_ctrl, eye_target_ctrl, eye_jnt (indexed in eyes order).

Bases: Component

Base class for FK/IK limbs; subclasses set parts and bones.

The first bones + 1 guides are the IK chain, from the root to the IK end; the rest follow the IK end joint. Three joint chains are built from the guides and blended by fkIk: the FK chain (an FkRig), the IK chain and the blend chain, which the skin joints follow. The joints between the root and the IK end are hinges: their FK controls only bend around X.

The IK control moves the limb's end (end, e.g. a FootRoll), and ik_solver solves the IK chain onto it. The pole vector hangs from pole_rotate_ctrl, a ring on the line from the limb's root to the IK control: turning it swings the pole around that line. The pole sits on the bend side of the first hinge joint; the pole control itself is hidden until poleVis on the ring is on.

Roles: settings_ctrl, ik_ctrl, pole_rotate_ctrl, pole_ctrl, bend_ctrl (indexed when there are two or more hinges), <label>_bendy_ctrl, ik_handle, ik_jnt, blend_jnt, jnt (indexed), <part>_twist_jnt (indexed), fk_rig (the FkRig's root), plus the solver's and the end's.

A neck joint from guide to head_guide, turned by one control.

head_space follows the control at the head guide; parent the head there. Attributes on the root (not keyable) take their values from the head; Head connects them when given the neck:

  • headTwist (0): spread along the twist joints.
  • fkIk (0): 0 turns the neck joint with the control (FK); 1 aims it at head_target and stretches or shortens it so its tip reaches the target (IK). The aim keeps the control's X axis as its up, so the control still turns and twists the neck.
  • minStretch (0.9), maxStretch (1.1): in IK, the neck's length stays between these times its rest length, so the tip may stop short of the target. FK ignores them.

head_target sits at the head guide under head_space; constrain it to whatever the neck should reach for, e.g. the head control.

Roles: ctrl, jnt, tip_jnt, head_space, head_target, twist_jnt (indexed), bendy_ctrl.

Bases: Component

Spine joints spread evenly along a curve shaped by the pelvis, middle and chest controls.

Controls:

  • pelvis_ctrl moves and turns the hips; the curve's base follows it.
  • fk_ctrls (at the pelvis and the middle) turn everything above them.
  • chest_ctrl, under the FK controls, moves and turns the chest.
  • mid_ctrl rides the middle of the spine and bends it.

The chest can't pull away from or push into the pelvis past minStretch and maxStretch on the chest control (0.9 and 1.1 times the pelvis-to-chest length): chest_space turns with the chest control but stops there, and the spine and the chest joint follow it. Parent what hangs from the chest (clavicles, neck) under chest_space.

The curve's CVs follow the controls like a skinned curve (the ones next to the middle are 70/30 blends), and a second curve beside it gives the joints their up direction, so the spine twists with the controls. The joints stay spread evenly, so the spine stretches with the curve. This is pkrig3's torso, so its skin weights carry over.

Skin joints: pelvis_jnt > 1_jnt... > chest_jnt > chest_tip_jnt.

Roles: pelvis_ctrl, fk_ctrl (indexed), mid_ctrl, chest_ctrl, chest_space, pelvis_jnt, jnt (indexed), chest_jnt, chest_tip_jnt, curve, up_curve.
