pkrig4.twist

twist

Swing/twist decomposition with quaternion nodes.

Example:

from pkrig4 import twist

nodes = twist.extract_swing_twist(elbow_tg_ori, "y")
elbow_tg_ori.twist >> sleeve.ctrl.rz      # twist around Y, in degrees

def extract_swing_twist(transform: Transform | str, axis: str = 'y') -> SwingTwist

Split transform's rotation into twist around axis and the remaining swing.

Adds two attributes to transform and drives them: twist (degrees around axis) and swing (a double3 rotation without the twist). Rotate order is respected.

Raises
ValueError
axis is not "x", "y" or "z".
