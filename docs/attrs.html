pkrig4.attrs

attrs

Channel-box helpers: separators, visibility switches, driven keys.

Example:

from pkrig4 import attrs

attrs.add_separator(head_ctrl, "expressions") attrs.add_visibility_switch(main.meta, "jacket", ["jacket_GRP"]) attrs.set_driven_keys(smile.weight, head_ctrl.expression, [(0, 0), (1, 1)])
